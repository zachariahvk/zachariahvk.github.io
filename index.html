About

Most marketing copy fails because nobody writing it has ever had to close anything.

I came up through content — three years at Matrix Comsec writing launch campaigns, video scripts, social and newsletter work for a technical product line. Then I moved into B2B sales at TranZact, where I spent my days on demos, objections and partner conversations.

That combination is the whole point. I know what a buyer actually asks before they commit, which means I write copy that answers it instead of talking around it. I'm looking for content marketing roles where that's useful.
